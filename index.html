Research

Enhancing Stress and Anxiety Detection Accuracy Through Multimodal Sensor Fusion and Advanced Machine Learning Techniques BIDA 2025 · Springer

First author, with Dev Sheth, Vinay Dubey, Aaditi Jadwani and K. M. Yogesh (M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences). Published in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics: Proceedings of BIDA 2025, Volume 2, Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies vol. 454, pp. 277–295.

A method for predicting stress and anxiety levels from wearable-device signals: accelerometer (ACC), skin temperature and electrodermal activity (EDA). Machine learning models classify stress and anxiety states from features extracted from these signals, enabling real-time, non-invasive monitoring.

  • Decision Tree (best overall): 93.77% accuracy, 94.01% precision, 94.29% recall
  • K-Nearest Neighbors: 92.48% accuracy, 89.41% precision, 95.24% recall

The results show these techniques work for non-invasive stress detection, a step toward early intervention and personalised mental-health monitoring in daily life.

Read it on Springer ↗ · DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-07837-7_22
